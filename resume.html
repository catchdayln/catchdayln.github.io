UNIVERSITY PROGRAM MANAGEMENT

Portland State University

Full-time

At PSU, I initially joined as Program Support for the inaugural year of the Young Southeast Asian Leaders Initiative (YSEALI). My responsibilities included aiding in the development and implementation of the program, coordinating logistics, supporting students, and ensuring the seamless execution of all program activities. These contributions were instrumental in establishing a robust foundation for the program's ongoing success.

I provided essential guidance in an advisory role to both prospective and current students, assisting them in navigating academic requirements and application procedures, thereby enhancing their overall educational experience. Additionally, I represented the department at networking events, actively participated in recruitment efforts, and coordinated the hiring of key faculty for the department's various programs.

During my tenure, I successfully guided the graduate programs through a significant transitional period. My efforts in streamlining operations, fostering cross-departmental collaboration, and upholding high standards of student support were pivotal to the department's operational efficiency and long-term success.

INTERNATIONAL PROGRAM COORDINATION

University of Oregon: AEI

Full-time

By leveraging a range of virtual learning tools and methods, I successfully maintained high engagement levels among students and preserved the Institute's commitment to excellence in language education despite unprecedented disruptions.

I coordinated events, managed digital marketing campaigns, and provided comprehensive student services to support AEI's diverse student body. These efforts not only helped sustain the Institute's day-to-day operations but also enhanced our community's resilience and overall impact.
