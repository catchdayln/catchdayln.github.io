Having experience and connections within the outdoor event community, I was contracted to help Montbell create its first America-based events right here in Oregon. As a result of our success in this area, Montbell later recruited me to be a part of their marketing team at the corporate office in Japan, where I learned about the various distinctions between American and Japanese marketing methods, as well as the importance Montbell placed on community and service. My time abroad allowed me to observe the differences and similarities of intercultural markets. This experience also honed my ability to create marketing content that would better relate the Montbell corporation to an American audience. This international experience equipped me with the skills to cooperate in a cross-cultural context and adapt to the environments with a variety of languages, cultures and workflows.
