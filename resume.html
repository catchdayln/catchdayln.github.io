WORKING ABROAD
EVENT COORDINATION

Montbell (Japan)

Contracted / Full-time

Montbell Co. Ltd. was established in Osaka, Japan with one goal in mind: produce high quality outdoor equipment. Since then the company has expanded its services and reach, covering much of Europe, Switzerland, and most recently the United States. With this expansion, Montbell was eager to raise awareness about their brand and products and wanted to do so by recreating their well established outdoor event series: Sea to Summit. These events separate Montbell from its competitors by demonstrating their commitment to outdoor exploration and adventure for all.

Having experience and connections within the outdoor event community, I was contracted to help Montbell create its first America-based events right here in Oregon. Duties included event promotion, the creation of branding and working as an intermediary between Montbell Japan, the event hosting companies, as well as various vendors, sponsors, travel agencies and local governments. I served as their marketing lead for the company as we developed new outdoor events hosted by Montbell and also worked to find new ways to involve Montbell within local communities and already established events.

As a result of our success in this area, Montbell later recruited me to be a part of their marketing team at the corporate office in Japan, where I learned about the various distinctions between American and Japanese marketing methods, as well as the importance Montbell placed on community and service. My time abroad allowed me to observe the differences and similarities of intercultural markets. This experience also honed my ability to create marketing content that would better relate the Montbell corporation to an American audience. This international experience equipped me with the skills to cooperate in a cross-cultural context and adapt to the environments with a variety of languages, cultures and workflows.
